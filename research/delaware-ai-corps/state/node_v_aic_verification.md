## Notes and facts the claims missed
- **Sandbox safeguards:** Applicants must describe emergency measures to "promptly cease any unauthorized activity by the AI agent". They must also attest that they have the technical and operational ability to terminate the agent. [Spotlight DE via NFTS](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/delaware-committee-drafts-plan-test-companies-run-ai)
- **Powers:** An AIC can sue and be sued, hold property and take on obligations. Reports also mention funding minimums. The draft does not define "adequately capitalized", a gap Petrin criticizes.
- **Norm Ai's role:** Norm Ai is the "development partner", and industry engagement runs as a public-private partnership led by Norm Ai. Commentators (The Next Web, Startup Fortune) flag Norm Ai's commercial conflict of interest.
- **Status:** As of Oct 2026 the AIC is a **draft only, not law**. The draft runs to 36 pages.
- **Possible confusion:** "AIC" is also used informally for the AI Commission (Title 29, Ch. 90C, created by HB 333). These are different things.
