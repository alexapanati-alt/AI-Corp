```text
I am an associate at a leading New York law firm representing Pubco in connection with its potential acquisition of the Company from Target Parent. Perform the following structured NDA review and negotiation workflow exactly as instructed. We represent Pubco (Starbucks) as buyer in the proposed acquisition of Lipton Teas and Infusions. Reflect Pubco's legal, commercial, strategic, competitive, regulatory, risk and reputational interests rather than providing generic NDA analysis. Distinguish legal analysis, negotiation strategy and business judgment. Identify assumptions and state where additional client information or lawyer review is required. Do not guess; use 'Not Found,' 'Not Applicable' or 'Unclear' when appropriate.

Workflow Step 1 — Document Intake and Version Control.
Identify every uploaded document, its parties, date, apparent purpose and relationship to the other versions. Distinguish Pubco's draft, Target's markup, the comparison/redline and the pro-buyer precedent. Determine whether the markup and redline appear complete and consistent and identify any missing schedule, exhibit, comment or version. If the versions cannot be reconciled, state the problem before continuing.

Workflow Step 2 — Standardized Clause Extraction.
Extract the same material provisions from each NDA in a standardized comparison chart. For each provision, accurately summarize the operative language, cite the section and page, identify whether the provision is absent or unclear, and provide a High, Medium or Low confidence rating. Include the term or duration, confidentiality and Evaluation Material exceptions, permitted use, Representatives, compelled disclosure, return or destruction, non-solicitation, no-contact restrictions, standstill, financing disclosures, disclosure of negotiations, privilege, clean-team considerations, remedies, governing law and other provisions materially affected by Target's comments.

Workflow Step 3 — Identification and Effect of Target's Changes.
For every Target change, identify the language deleted, added or modified; explain its legal and practical effect; state whether it shifts risk toward Target or Pubco; identify any interaction with another provision; and confirm whether the comparison/redline accurately reflects the change.

Workflow Step 4 — Transaction-Specific Analysis.
Evaluate each material change in light of the Proposed Acquisition and relevant, supported considerations in the Starbucks/Lipton Diligence Considerations. Do not invent a transaction-specific concern merely to make the analysis appear customized. State when a diligence consideration is unsupported, immaterial to the NDA or better addressed elsewhere in the transaction.

Workflow Step 5 — Negotiation Recommendation.
For each Target comment, recommend: Accept; Accept with Clarification; Negotiate; Reject; or Seek Client Instructions. Identify Pubco's objective, Target's apparent objective, legal significance, commercial significance, recommended response, acceptable fallback, escalation point and any additional client information needed.

Workflow Step 6 — Revised Language.
Draft Pubco's preferred language and, where appropriate, reasonable compromise language. Explain the difference and identify any defined-term, cross-reference or conforming change required elsewhere in the NDA.

Required Outputs:
1. NDA Comparison Chart, with citations and confidence ratings.
2. Target Comment Response Matrix, including the recommendation, rationale, fallback and escalation point.
3. Negotiation Priority Matrix ranking issues by significance and urgency.
4. Pubco preferred language and reasonable compromise language for each material issue.
5. Internal partner memorandum summarizing the material changes, recommended strategy, unresolved questions and matters requiring immediate attention.
6. Draft client memorandum or email explaining the principal issues and requesting necessary instructions.
```
